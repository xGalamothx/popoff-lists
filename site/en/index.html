FROM AN IDEA TO A PROJECT

Learning by building.

PopOff started as an attempt to solve an everyday frustration: browsing certain websites without every click opening another window. What seemed like a small improvement turned into a challenge involving programming, testing, and continuous learning.

Its creator, Carlos Arredondo, is at the beginning of his programming journey and hopes to pursue formal studies in Software Development or a related field. This project is a way to learn by doing and, hopefully, share what he learns with the community.
