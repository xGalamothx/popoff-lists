COMMUNITY

Build, learn, and share.

PopOff is a personal, non-profit project. Ideas, feedback, and words of encouragement all help it grow.

How can you help?

Share your experience

Tell others what works for you and what could be improved.

Report an issue

Describe your browser, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it. Avoid sharing private data or URLs containing tokens.

Share knowledge

The documentation is intended for students and developers interested in learning about browser extensions and privacy.

Offer voluntary support

If you feel like it, you can buy the creator a coffee. There are no fees or exclusive benefits.

About the creator

I'm Carlos, a technology enthusiast with basic programming knowledge and a lot of motivation to learn. I'm working on PopOff as an independent project, and I'd like to pursue formal studies in Software Development or a related field. I'm not a big company: I'm learning as I go while trying to create something that may be useful to others.

Support the project

All contributions are voluntary. PopOff will remain free, and a donation does not unlock features or change access to the tool.
